§ what's new · v0.2.0

Claaky leaves the sidebar and moves onto your desktop.

Version 0.2.0 is the biggest update yet: a desktop companion you can ask anything without opening the IDE, a cosy 3D room where Claaky shows what the agent is doing, and Kimi 3.1 Pro in the model list.

Desktop companion 3D room Kimi 3.1 Pro
01 · desktop companion

A little helper that floats above your work.

Claaky lives on your desktop, always on top. Drag him anywhere: his position is remembered. He reacts in real time to the seven states of the agent: idle, thinking, working, planning, done, error and sleeping.

  • ✓Click Claaky to open a frosted-glass chat bubble. It opens to the left when he sits on the right half of the screen.
  • ✓Ask mode answers directly, without tools and without opening Claake Code.
  • ✓Code mode hands the task to Claake Code and opens it if needed.
  • ✓Paste text or capture an area of the screen (macOS), then ask about it.
  • ✓The companion keeps its own history. Continue in Claake Code picks a conversation up in the IDE.
shortcut

⌃ ⌥ Space

A global shortcut opens the bubble from any app. You can change it in Settings; the old Alt+Space shortcut is migrated automatically.

always there

Menu bar and Workspace

Show or hide Claaky from the menu bar icon or the Claaky button in the Workspace. Closing the main window keeps him running; Cmd+Q quits.

settings

Yours to tune

Choose the shortcut, the default mode, which models the companion may use, and whether it starts when you log in.

02 · Claaky's 3D room

See what the agent does, at a glance.

Inside the IDE, Claaky now has his own animated 3D room, with warm, cosy lighting and a layout faithful to the original illustration. Every agent state has a place in the room.

  • ✓He types at his desk while the agent works, thinks in front of the whiteboard, and sleeps under a blanket when nothing happens.
  • ✓He teleports between spots with a soft fade, instead of walking across the room.
  • ✓When a test fails, the test machine flashes orange.
  • ✓Lighter than before: 7 animations instead of 20, and 3 MB of images instead of 13 MB.
  • ✓Images load only when needed, and only the part of the scene that changes is redrawn.
